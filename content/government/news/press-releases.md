# Municipal News & Development Updates — Obando

> Public reporting on local governance milestones, community programs, infrastructure achievements, and cultural activities in Obando, Bulacan.

---

## Overview

The **Public Information Office (PIO)** of the Municipality of Obando documents and reports major municipal milestones, social welfare initiatives, infrastructure progress, and cultural events under the leadership of **Municipal Mayor Leonardo "Ding" D. Valeda** and **Vice Mayor Rowell S. Rillera**.

---

## Recent Civic & Infrastructure Highlights

### 1. Flood Control Dike Rehabilitation & Estero Dredging

The Municipal Government, in coordination with the DPWH Bulacan 1st District Engineering Office, has completed key dredging operations along critical bottleneck waterways of the Obando and Binuangan river networks. Automated sluice gates in Brgy. Tawiran and Brgy. Paliwas have undergone mechanical upgrades to prevent tidal water backflow during astronomical peak tides.

### 2. Modernization of the Obando Rural Health Unit (RHU)

The Obando RHU has expanded its laboratory and diagnostic capabilities, adding daily rapid diagnostic testing, blood chemistry monitoring, and an increased inventory of free maintenance medicines for senior citizens enrolled in the municipal hypertension and diabetes clubs.

### 3. Annual Municipal Scholarship Grants Release

Over one thousand college undergraduates from indigent, fisherfolk, and solo-parent households across Obando's 11 barangays received their semestral educational assistance grants at the Obando Municipal Gymnasium to support their tertiary education tuition, transportation, and books.

### 4. Coastal Livelihood and Fisherfolk Support

In partnership with the Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR Region III), the Municipal Agriculture Office distributed high-yield saline tilapia fingerlings and bangus fry to accredited coastal fisherfolk associations in Brgy. Binuangan and Brgy. Salambao, alongside water quality monitoring kits to prevent seasonal fish mortality.

### 5. Heritage and Cultural Tourism Promotion

The local government actively preserves the town's world-famous **Sayaw sa Obando** fertility dance traditions. Municipal coordination committees work with the Diocesan Shrine of Nuestra Señora de Salambao and San Pascual Baylon Parish to ensure orderly reception, medical assistance, and safety for the tens of thousands of pilgrims arriving every May 17, 18, and 19.
