# Official Municipal Advisories & Coastal Bulletins — Obando

> Verified public advisories, tidal flood alerts, class suspensions, and community service updates from the Municipality of Obando.

---

## Overview

The **Municipality of Obando**, through the **Public Information Office (PIO)** and the **Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (MDRRMO)**, issues official public advisories to protect citizens, commuters, and businesses from hazard risks and keep the public informed of municipal operations.

All official advisories are authenticated and released through the Office of the Municipal Mayor.

---

## Coastal High-Tide & Weather Advisories

Due to Obando's direct proximity to Manila Bay and tidal river channels, the MDRRMO broadcasts daily high-tide forecasts based on NAMRIA astronomical projections:

- **Tidal Heights Above 1.4m:** Gutter-deep waters on low-lying sections of J.P. Rizal St. (Brgy. Paliwas, Tawiran, Panghulo). Light vehicles and tricycles are advised to use caution or take alternate elevated routes.
- **Tidal Heights Above 1.6m – 1.8m:** Knee-deep to thigh-deep street flooding during peak tide hours. Preemptive sandbagging and parking elevation required.
- **Tropical Cyclone Weather Bulletins:** Real-time updates during PAGASA tropical depression, storm, and typhoon passages over Southern Central Luzon.

---

## Class and Work Suspension Protocols

Under DepEd Order No. 037, s. 2022 and Municipal Executive Orders:

1. **Automatic Suspension:** When PAGASA raises Tropical Cyclone Wind Signal (TCWS) No. 1, 2, or 3 over Bulacan, in-person classes in affected levels are automatically suspended without requiring a local executive order.
2. **Localized Flood Suspensions:** During extreme tidal surges or heavy monsoon rainfall without a wind signal, the **Municipal Mayor** announces localized class or government work suspensions.
   - **Morning Session Announcements:** Released on or before **4:30 AM**.
   - **Afternoon Session Announcements:** Released on or before **10:30 AM**.

## Municipal Service Holiday Schedules & Traffic Advisories

- **Business Permit Renewal (January BOSS):** Extended service hours and continuous noon-break service at the Municipal Hall during the month of January.
- **Town Fiesta Traffic Scheme (May 17–19):** Special one-way traffic routing along J.P. Rizal St. and designated pilgrim parking zones near the Obando Church during the Feast of San Pascual Baylon, Santa Clara, and Nuestra Señora de Salambao.
- **Undas Traffic & Cemetery Regulations (November 1–2):** Traffic re-routing and strict ban on bladed weapons, alcohol, and loud sound systems at the Obando Municipal Cemetery.
