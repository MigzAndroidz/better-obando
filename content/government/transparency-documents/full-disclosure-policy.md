# Full Disclosure Policy (FDP) Portal — Obando

> Public access to mandatory financial, budgetary, and procurement disclosures of the Municipality of Obando under DILG guidelines.

---

## Overview

In compliance with **DILG Memorandum Circular No. 2010-83** and subsequent issuances on the **Full Disclosure Policy (FDP)**, the **Municipality of Obando** publicly discloses its financial transactions, annual budgets, quarterly expenditures, and procurement notices.

The Full Disclosure Policy aims to promote good financial housekeeping, combat corruption, and ensure that every peso of public funds generated from local taxes and the National Tax Allotment (NTA) is accounted for and accessible to every citizen of Obando.

---

## Mandatory Disclosure Documents

The following official financial documents are posted quarterly and annually on the **Full Disclosure Policy Physical Board** at the Obando Municipal Hall and the national **DILG FDP Portal** (fdpp.dilg.gov.ph):

### 1. Annual Financial Documents:

- **Annual Budget Report:** Summary of the approved General Fund and Special Education Fund (SEF) executive budget enacted by the Sangguniang Bayan.
- **Annual Statement of Receipts and Expenditures (SRE):** Certified revenue performance report showing local tax collections, business fees, and national allotment shares.
- **Annual Procurement Plan (APP):** Detailed itemized schedule of goods, civil infrastructure works, and consulting services programmed for competitive bidding by the Bids and Awards Committee (BAC).

### 2. Quarterly Financial Disclosures:

- **Quarterly Statement of Cash Flows:** Inflows and outflows of cash reserves across all municipal bank accounts.
- **Special Education Fund (SEF) Utilization Report:** Quarterly breakdown of expenses allocated to public elementary and secondary schools, teachers' allowances, and classroom repairs.
- **Trust Fund Utilization Report:** Tracking of national government grants, calamity relief subsidies, and inter-agency transfers.
- **20% Component Development Fund Utilization:** Capital expenditures dedicated to infrastructure, flood control dikes, and social development.
- **Local Disaster Risk Reduction & Management Fund (LDRRMF) Report:** Expenditures for emergency rescue gear, relief food pack pre-positioning, and flood mitigation.

### 3. Public Procurement Disclosures:

- **Bid Results of Civil Works, Goods, and Services:** Winning contractors, bid prices, and awarded contracts.
- **Abstract of Bids as Calculated:** Complete comparative evaluation of supplier bids processed by the Obando Bids and Awards Committee (BAC).

## Where to Inspect Physical Transparency Boards

In addition to digital postings, physical transparency boards displaying the certified DILG FDP reports are maintained at:

1. **Obando Municipal Hall Main Lobby:** J.P. Rizal St., Brgy. Paliwas (Adjacent to the Treasury Cashier).
2. **Obando Public Market Notice Board:** Entrance concourse of the public market.
3. **Barangay Hall Transparency Boards:** Maintained in each of the 11 barangays for barangay-level financial disclosure.
