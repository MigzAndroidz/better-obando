# Obando Citizen's Charter (Piagam ng Mamamayan)

> Official frontline service standards, processing timetables, documentary requirements, and citizen feedback mechanisms under Republic Act 11032.

---

## Overview

In accordance with **Republic Act 11032 (Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act of 2018)** and the guidelines of the **Anti-Red Tape Authority (ARTA)**, the **Municipality of Obando** maintains an updated **Citizen's Charter**.

The Citizen's Charter serves as a binding social contract between the local government unit and the citizens of Obando, detailing the step-by-step procedure, required documents, responsible office personnel, fees, and standard processing times for every frontline municipal service.

---

## Service Standards and Processing Timelines

Under RA 11032, all municipal government transactions in Obando adhere to strict statutory turnaround times upon submission of complete requirements:

- **Simple Transactions:** Processed within **three (3) working days** (e.g., Community Tax Certificate / Cedula, Barangay Clearance, certified copies of civil registry documents, burial permits).
- **Complex Transactions:** Processed within **seven (7) working days** (e.g., new business permits, locational clearances, scholarship evaluation, AICS medical assistance vouchers).
- **Highly Technical Transactions:** Processed within **twenty (20) working days** (e.g., building permits involving multi-disciplinary structural and MEP engineering reviews, subdivision approvals).

---

## Zero-Contact Policy & No Noon Break

- **No Noon Break Policy:** In compliance with the Civil Service Commission (CSC) guidelines, all frontline windows at the Obando Municipal Hall (including the Municipal Treasury Cashier, BPLO, Local Civil Registry, and MSWDO) maintain continuous skeletal staffing through lunch hours (12:00 PM – 1:00 PM) to ensure uninterrupted service to transacting residents.
- **Anti-Fixer Warning:** Section 21 of RA 11032 strictly penalizes fixing. Transactions should only be conducted with authorized municipal employees wearing official identification cards at designated service counters. Never engage unauthorized fixers outside the Municipal Hall.

---

## Frontline Service Directories by Department

The complete Citizen's Charter handbook is available for public inspection at the Public Assistance and Complaints Desk (PACD) at the entrance of the Obando Municipal Hall, covering:

1. **Office of the Municipal Mayor & BPLO:** Business permits, special permits, solemnization of marriage.
2. **Municipal Treasury Office:** Real property tax (amilyar), transfer taxes, business taxes, cedula.
3. **Local Civil Registry Office:** Birth registration, marriage licenses, death registration, legitimation.
4. **Municipal Social Welfare & Development Office:** Crisis financial aid (AICS), senior citizen IDs, PWD IDs, solo parent IDs.
5. **Municipal Planning & Development Office:** Locational clearance, zoning certifications.
6. **Municipal Engineering Office:** Building permits, electrical wiring inspection, occupancy certificates.
7. **Municipal Health Office / RHU:** Medical consultations, maternal checkups, immunizations, sanitary permits.
8. **Municipal Environment & Natural Resources Office:** Garbage schedules, environmental clearances.
