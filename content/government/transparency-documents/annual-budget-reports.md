# Annual Executive Budget and Municipal Appropriations — Obando

> Public summary of the General Fund budget, revenue allocations, capital outlays, and social development expenditures of Obando, Bulacan.

---

## Overview

The **Annual Executive Budget** of the Municipality of Obando embodies the municipal government's development priorities, fiscal policies, and socio-economic agenda for each fiscal calendar year (January 1 to December 31).

Prepared by the **Local Finance Committee (LFC)**—comprising the Municipal Planning and Development Coordinator, the Municipal Budget Officer, and the Municipal Treasurer—under the direction of the **Municipal Mayor**, the budget is officially submitted to the **Sangguniang Bayan** for legislative scrutiny, public hearings, and enactment through an **Appropriation Ordinance**.

---

## Sources of Municipal Revenue

Obando's municipal budget is funded through two primary fiscal streams:

### 1. Locally Generated Revenues:

- **Local Business Taxes (LBT):** Paid by commercial enterprises, fishpond businesses, banks, and retailers operating in Obando.
- **Real Property Tax (Amilyar):** Basic tax on land, buildings, machinery, and agricultural/aquaculture land, divided equally between the General Fund and the Special Education Fund.
- **Regulatory Fees and User Charges:** Mayor's permit fees, building permit fees, sanitary inspection fees, market stall rentals, and cemetery burial fees.

### 2. External Revenue Sources:

- **National Tax Allotment (NTA - formerly Internal Revenue Allotment / IRA):** Obando's statutory share of national government tax collections under the Mandanas-Garcia Supreme Court ruling.
- **National Government Subsidies & Grants:** Special allocations from the Local Government Support Fund (LGSF), disaster funds, and Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) flood mitigation programs.

## Budget Review and Audit Oversight

- **Provincial Review:** In accordance with the Local Government Code, the enacted municipal appropriation ordinance is submitted to the **Sangguniang Panlalawigan ng Bulacan** for administrative review and confirmation of compliance with budgetary limitations.
- **Commission on Audit (COA):** All disbursements, payrolls, and public contracts are subject to post-audit by the resident **COA State Auditor** stationed at the Obando Municipal Hall. Annual Audit Reports (AAR) are submitted to the Office of the President and publicly posted on the COA official portal.
