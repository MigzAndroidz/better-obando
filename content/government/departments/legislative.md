# Sangguniang Bayan ng Obando (Legislative Department)

> The municipal legislative council of Obando, Bulacan, responsible for enacting municipal ordinances, passing local resolutions, and approving the municipal annual budget.

---

## Overview

The **Sangguniang Bayan of Obando** is the legislative branch of the municipal government. Governed by Republic Act 7160 (The Local Government Code of 1991), the municipal council is composed of the **Municipal Vice Mayor as Presiding Officer**, eight (8) regular councilors elected at large, the President of the Liga ng mga Barangay (ABC President / Ex-Officio), and the President of the Sangguniang Kabataan (SK) Municipal Federation (Ex-Officio).

The Sangguniang Bayan convenes for its **Regular Session every week** at the Sangguniang Bayan Session Hall, 2nd Floor, Obando Municipal Hall, Brgy. Paliwas.

## Key Legislative Powers and Mandates

Under Section 447 of the Local Government Code, the Sangguniang Bayan performs the following essential duties:

### 1. Enactment of Municipal Ordinances

Debates, drafts, and enacts binding local ordinances governing public safety, environmental protection, solid waste segregation, zoning, traffic management, and revenue generation.

### 2. Approval of the Annual Executive Budget

Reviews, deliberates, and enacts the annual General Fund Budget and Supplemental Budgets of the Municipality of Obando, ensuring public revenues from the National Tax Allotment (NTA) and local collections are responsibly allocated to essential social services and flood mitigation.

### 3. Review of Barangay Ordinances

Examines and reviews all ordinances and financial appropriations passed by the 11 Barangay Councils to ensure consistency with national laws and municipal codes.

### 4. Public Hearings and Consultations

Conducts multi-stakeholder public hearings with business owners, fisherfolk, transport groups, and homeowners before passing tax codes, fare adjustments, or zoning revisions.

## Public Sessions and Citizen Participation

- **Regular Session Schedule:** Weekly regular sessions held at the Sangguniang Bayan Session Hall.
- **Public Attendance:** Sangguniang Bayan sessions are generally open to the public, fostering transparency in local governance.
- **Citizen Petitions and Letters:** Citizens, civic organizations, and homeowners' associations may submit written petitions, memorandums, or legislative suggestions addressed to the **Office of the Secretary to the Sangguniang Bayan**.

---

## Requesting Copies of Municipal Ordinances and Resolutions

Citizens, researchers, and business owners requiring certified true copies of enacted municipal ordinances (e.g., Municipal Revenue Code, Market Code, Solid Waste Management Ordinance, Zoning Regulations):

1. **Submit Request:** File a document request slip at the **Office of the Secretary to the Sangguniang Bayan**, 2nd Floor, Obando Municipal Hall, Paliwas.
2. **Specify Details:** State the ordinance number, year, or subject matter.
3. **Pay Certification Fee:** Settle the documentary stamp and certification fee at the Municipal Treasury Cashier.
4. **Claim Certified Copy:** Collect the authenticated document bearing the official seal of the Sangguniang Bayan.
