# Municipal Treasury Office (MTO)

> Financial and revenue collection arm responsible for local tax assessment, real property taxes (amilyar), and municipal treasury management.

---

## Mandate & Overview

The **Municipal Treasury Office (MTO)** of Obando manages the financial resources, local revenue collection, and fund disbursements of the municipal government pursuant to the Local Government Code of 1991. The office assesses and collects local taxes, regulatory fees, market stall rentals, and real property taxes, ensuring fiscal sustainability for public services.

- **Office Head:** Municipal Treasurer
- **Location:** Ground Floor, Obando Municipal Hall, J.P. Rizal St., Brgy. Paliwas, Obando, Bulacan 3021
- **Service Hours:** Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
- **Trunkline:** (02) 8292-2043

---

## Frontline Services

1. **Real Property Tax (Amilyar) Payment:** Assessment and collection of annual basic real property tax and Special Education Fund (SEF) tax on land, buildings, and fishponds. (Discounts up to 20% available for prompt full-year payments in January).
2. **Community Tax Certificate (Cedula / CTC):** Issuance of individual and corporate community tax certificates required for legal transactions, employment, and business permits.
3. **Local Business Tax (LBT) Collection:** Computation and receipting of annual or quarterly business taxes based on BPLO tax orders of payment.
4. **Market Stall Rental & Franchise Fees:** Collection of monthly lease fees for public market stallholders and municipal tricycle franchise fees.
5. **Municipal Tax Clearance:** Issuance of certification that a property or business has no outstanding tax liabilities with the municipal government.

---

## Quick Transaction Checklist

- **For Real Property Tax:** Present the previous year's Official Receipt (OR) or latest Tax Declaration number.
- **For Community Tax Certificate (Cedula):** Valid ID and declaration of gross earnings/salaries for the preceding calendar year.
