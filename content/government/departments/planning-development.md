# Municipal Planning and Development Office (MPDO)

> The chief technical planning and zoning body formulating Obando's comprehensive development and land-use plans.

---

## Mandate & Overview

The **Municipal Planning and Development Office (MPDO)** of Obando serves as the central socio-economic, spatial, and infrastructure planning agency of the municipal government. The office formulates the Comprehensive Land Use Plan (CLUP), Comprehensive Development Plan (CDP), and the Annual Investment Program (AIP), while enforcing municipal zoning regulations through the Office of the Zoning Administrator.

- **Office Head:** Municipal Planning and Development Coordinator (MPDC) / Zoning Administrator
- **Location:** 2nd Floor, Obando Municipal Hall, J.P. Rizal St., Brgy. Paliwas, Obando, Bulacan 3021
- **Service Hours:** Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
- **Trunkline:** (02) 8292-2043

---

## Frontline Services

1. **Locational Clearance (Zoning):** Mandatory municipal clearance certifying that a proposed residential, commercial, or industrial construction complies with the municipal zoning ordinance.
2. **Zoning Certification:** Formal certification of land use classification for land titling, property sales, or financial loan applications.
3. **Comprehensive Land Use Plan (CLUP) Assistance:** Providing mapping and demographic data to researchers, investors, and community organizations.
4. **Local Housing Board Secretariat:** Screening informal settler profiling and public resettlement initiatives along river easements.
5. **Municipal Project Monitoring:** Technical inspection and progress reporting for LGU infrastructure capital projects.

## Quick Transaction Checklist

- **For Locational Clearance:** Completed application form, Certified True Copy of TCT, latest Real Property Tax Declaration & Receipt, 5 sets of architectural site development plans, and Barangay Construction Clearance.
