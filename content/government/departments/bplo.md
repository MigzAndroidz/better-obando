# Business Permits and Licensing Office (BPLO)

> Frontline regulatory and economic arm responsible for business registration, licensing, and commercial operations in Obando.

---

## Mandate & Overview

The **Business Permits and Licensing Office (BPLO)** of Obando serves as the central administrative agency regulating all commercial, industrial, and service establishments within the municipality. The office coordinates the annual **Business One-Stop Shop (BOSS)** in January, integrating clearances from the Municipal Treasury, Engineering, Sanitary Health, MENRO, and the Bureau of Fire Protection.

- **Office Head:** BPLO Officer-in-Charge
- **Location:** Ground Floor, Obando Municipal Hall, J.P. Rizal St., Brgy. Paliwas, Obando, Bulacan 3021
- **Service Hours:** Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM (No noon break during January BOSS)
- **Trunkline:** (02) 8292-2043

---

## Frontline Services

1. **New Mayor's Business Permit Application:** Issuance of business permits to new sole proprietorships, partnerships, corporations, and cooperatives operating in Obando.
2. **Annual Business Permit Renewal:** Evaluation of gross sales declarations and processing of annual/quarterly permit renewals every January.
3. **Special & Seasonal Permits:** Permits for fiesta trade booths, seasonal bazaars, promotional motorcades, and public sound systems.
4. **Business Retirement & Closure:** Official inspection and processing of business cessation certificates to stop tax accruals.
5. **Occupational Permits:** Issuance of working permits and Mayor's clearance for commercial and service workers.

---

## Quick Transaction Checklist

- **For New Business:** DTI/SEC/CDA Registration, Host Barangay Business Clearance, Lease Contract or Title, CTC (Cedula), Occupancy Permit (if applicable).
- **For Renewal:** Previous Mayor's Permit & ORs, Current Barangay Clearance, Sworn Statement of Prior Year Gross Sales / AFS, Updated Health Cards.
