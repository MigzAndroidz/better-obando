# Executive Department — Municipality of Obando

> Official mandate, administrative leadership, and public service priorities of the Office of the Municipal Mayor and the Office of the Municipal Vice Mayor.

---

## Executive Leadership

The **Executive Department of the Municipality of Obando** is headed by the **Municipal Mayor**, who acts as the local chief executive of the municipal government. Working in close executive-legislative coordination with the **Municipal Vice Mayor**, the executive leadership directs municipal development, manages frontline public administration, implements enacted municipal ordinances, and ensures the efficient delivery of essential services to all residents across Obando's 11 barangays.

## Office of the Municipal Mayor

The Municipal Mayor exercises general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services, and activities of the municipal government pursuant to Section 444 of Republic Act 7160 (The Local Government Code of 1991).

### Core Executive Responsibilities:

1. **Policy Direction & Governance:** Enforces all national laws, provincial regulations, and municipal ordinances for the peace, order, and general welfare of Obando.
2. **Economic & Social Development:** Directs the preparation of the Executive Budget and Annual Investment Plan (AIP) to fund health centers, public education, flood infrastructure, and social assistance.
3. **Disaster Risk Governance:** Serves as Chairperson of the Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council (MDRRMC), leading emergency response, preemptive evacuation, and coastal relief operations.
4. **Business & Investment Promotion:** Fosters an investor-friendly environment through the Business Permits and Licensing Office (BPLO) and Business One-Stop Shop (BOSS) to stimulate local employment.
5. **Civic & Public Representation:** Represents the municipality in official inter-agency conferences, regional development councils, and cultural civic celebrations.

## Priority Executive Development Pillars

The current municipal administration focuses on key strategic pillars tailored to Obando's geographic and cultural identity:

- **Coastal Resilience & Flood Mitigation:** Aggressive desiltation of waterways, rehabilitation of tidal gates, and maintenance of protective dikes along the Obando and Binuangan rivers to shield residential and aquaculture zones from high-tide surges.
- **Universal Healthcare Access:** Upgrading the Obando Rural Health Unit (RHU), expanding free maintenance medicine distribution, and funding maternal and child immunization across all 11 barangay health stations.
- **Youth Empowerment & Educational Assistance:** Sustaining the Obando Municipal Scholarship Program, providing annual SEF school supplies kits to public school learners, and supporting community sports development.
- **Fisheries & Aquaculture Modernization:** Assisting coastal fisherfolk and fishpond operators with fingerling subsidies, technical aquaculture seminars, and marine ecological conservation.
- **Cultural Heritage Preservation:** Championing the historic Obando Fertility Dance Festival (May 17-19) and fostering sustainable cultural tourism centered on the Sanctuary of San Pascual Baylon and Nuestra Señora de Salambao.

## Office of the Municipal Vice Mayor

The **Municipal Vice Mayor** serves as the presiding officer of the **Sangguniang Bayan** and leads the legislative arm of the local government.

### Mandate and Functions:

- Presides over regular and special legislative sessions of the municipal council, signing all enacted ordinances and resolutions.
- Serves as **Acting Municipal Mayor** during the temporary absence, official travel, or incapacity of the local chief executive, ensuring continuous administrative operations.
- Leads citizen consultative meetings, public hearings on proposed municipal tax measures, and legislative committee reviews.

## Frontline Executive Administrative Offices

Citizens transacting with the executive department may coordinate directly with these frontline offices at the Municipal Hall in Brgy. Paliwas:

- **Office of the Municipal Administrator:** Coordinates inter-departmental operations and municipal personnel.
- **Business Permits & Licensing Office (BPLO):** Ground floor; handles new business registrations and annual renewals.
- **Municipal Treasury Office:** Ground floor; handles local tax payments, real property taxes (amilyar), and community tax certificates (cedula).
- **Municipal Social Welfare & Development Office (MSWDO):** Ground floor; manages AICS crisis assistance, OSCA senior citizen IDs, and PWD benefits.
- **Municipal Planning & Development Office (MPDO):** 2nd floor; manages zoning, locational clearances, and municipal masterplans.
- **Municipal Engineering Office (MEO):** 2nd floor; handles building permits, electrical clearances, and infrastructure maintenance.
- **Municipal Health Office / Rural Health Unit (RHU):** Adjacent compound; provides free consultations, immunizations, and medicines.
- **Municipal Environment & Natural Resources Office (MENRO):** Ground floor; manages solid waste schedules and environmental compliance.
