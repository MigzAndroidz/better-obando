# Municipal Health Office & Rural Health Unit (RHU)

> Primary public healthcare provider delivering free outpatient consultations, immunizations, maternal care, and community sanitation in Obando.

---

## Mandate & Overview

The **Municipal Health Office (MHO)** of Obando operates the **Obando Rural Health Unit (RHU)** and supervises the 11 Barangay Health Stations (BHS). Guided by Department of Health (DOH) standards and the Universal Health Care (UHC) Act, the RHU provides primary healthcare services, preventive health drives, maternal and newborn care, epidemiological disease surveillance, and environmental sanitation monitoring across the municipality.

## Frontline Services

1. **Free Outpatient Medical Consultations:** Daily general check-ups, diagnostic lab exams (CBC, Urinalysis, Blood Sugar, Sputum), and issuance of medical certificates.
2. **Free Maintenance & Essential Medicines:** Dispensing of hypertension and diabetes maintenance pills, antibiotics, and pediatric syrups from the RHU Botika.
3. **Maternal Care & Immunization (EPI):** Prenatal check-ups, micronutrient supplements, facility-based delivery endorsements, and free routine vaccines for infants every Wednesday.
4. **National Tuberculosis Program (TB-DOTS):** Free GeneXpert molecular testing and 6-month supervised anti-TB medication.
5. **Sanitary Permits & Health Cards:** Mandatory sanitary inspection of food establishments and medical clearance for commercial food handlers and market vendors.
6. **Animal Bite Treatment Center (ABTC):** Post-exposure anti-rabies vaccination and wound assessment for dog and cat bite cases.
