# Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)

> Social protection, crisis intervention, senior citizen welfare, solo parents assistance, and family development services in Obando.

---

## Mandate & Overview

The **Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)** of Obando is the primary government agency mandated to provide social protection, poverty alleviation programs, crisis intervention, and family empowerment to disadvantaged individuals, families, and communities. The office oversees the Office of Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA), the Persons with Disability Affairs Office (PDAO), and the 11 Barangay Child Development Centers (Daycare).

- **Office Head:** Municipal Social Welfare and Development Officer (MSWDO)
- **Location:** Ground Floor, Obando Municipal Hall, J.P. Rizal St., Brgy. Paliwas, Obando, Bulacan 3021
- **Service Hours:** Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM

## Frontline Services

1. **Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situations (AICS):** Emergency financial assistance for hospitalization, costly prescription drugs, funeral/burial costs, and transportation assistance for stranded indigents.
2. **Senior Citizen Services (OSCA):** Issuance of Senior Citizen IDs and purchase booklets for 20% discounts, social pension enrollment for indigent seniors, and centenarian cash awards.
3. **Persons with Disability (PWD) Services:** Issuance of PWD IDs, purchase discount booklets, and distribution of mobility assistive devices (wheelchairs, canes, crutches).
4. **Solo Parents Welfare Services:** Issuance of Solo Parent IDs under RA 11861, parental leave endorsements, and financial subsidy evaluations.
5. **Women and Children Protection Desk (WCPD Coordination):** Social case management, protective custody, and psychosocial counseling for victims of gender-based violence (RA 9262) and child abuse (RA 7610).
6. **Child Development Centers (Daycare):** Supervision of early childhood learning centers and supplementary feeding programs across all 11 barangays.
7. **Disaster Relief and Camp Coordination:** Distribution of family food packs and management of municipal evacuation centers during typhoons and high tides.

## Quick Transaction Checklist

- **For AICS Medical Aid:** Clinical Abstract / Medical Certificate, Hospital Billing or Prescription with quotation, Barangay Certificate of Indigency, and valid IDs.
- **For AICS Burial Aid:** Registered Death Certificate, Funeral Contract, Barangay Certificate of Indigency, and valid ID of claimant.
