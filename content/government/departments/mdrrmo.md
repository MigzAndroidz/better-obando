# Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (MDRRMO)

> 24/7 disaster monitoring, high-tide early warnings, emergency ambulance dispatch, and flood rescue operations in Obando.

---

## Mandate & Overview

The **Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (MDRRMO)** of Obando serves as the frontline disaster risk reduction, climate adaptation, and emergency response agency of the municipality. Operating under Republic Act 10121, the office maintains a 24/7 **Emergency Operations Center (EOC)** in Brgy. Paliwas, coordinating high-tide bulletins, river water level sensors, emergency medical services (EMS), and waterborne search-and-rescue teams.

- **Office Head:** MDRRM Officer
- **Location:** Emergency Operations Center, Obando Municipal Hall Compound, Brgy. Paliwas, Obando, Bulacan 3021
- **24/7 Rescue & Emergency Operations Center Hotline:** **(02) 8281-5816**
- **Trunkline:** (02) 8292-2043

---

## Frontline Services

1. **24/7 Emergency Medical Response & Ambulance Transport:** Rapid dispatch of paramedic-equipped ambulances for acute vehicular accidents, maternal emergencies, and critical hospital transfers.
2. **Real-Time Coastal High-Tide & Flood Bulletins:** Daily monitoring and public broadcasting of NAMRIA astronomical tide forecasts and PAGASA rainfall warnings to inform commuters, fishpond owners, and schools.
3. **Flood Search, Rescue & Preemptive Evacuation:** Deployment of motorized aluminum rescue boats and amphibious vehicles to evacuate stranded families in low-lying and coastal barangays (Binuangan, Salambao, Tawiran, Paliwas).
4. **Community Disaster Drills & First Aid Training:** Conducting basic life support (BLS), earthquake drills, and flood survival training for barangays, schools, and private enterprises.
5. **Accredited Community Disaster Volunteers (ACDV):** Organizing, equipping, and deploying trained civic volunteers during typhoons and major civic festivals (such as the Obando Fertility Dance Festival).

## Emergency Survival Checklist

- **Go-Bag Essentials:** Clean drinking water (3L per person for 3 days), ready-to-eat canned goods, flashlight, battery radio, whistle, first aid kit, 7-day maintenance medicines, and waterproof pouch for legal documents.
- **Emergency Hotline:** Memorize or post on your refrigerator: **(02) 8281-5816 (Obando Rescue 24/7)**.
