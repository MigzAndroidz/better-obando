# Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office (MENRO)

> Ecological solid waste management, river protection, coastal cleanups, and environmental ordinance enforcement in Obando.

---

## Mandate & Overview

The **Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office (MENRO)** of Obando is tasked with protecting the local environment, conserving natural resources, and implementing Republic Act 9003 (Ecological Solid Waste Management Act) and local environmental codes. Given Obando's vulnerable coastal river basin, MENRO coordinates residential and market garbage collection, monitors river waterways, organizes mangrove reforestation, and penalizes illegal dumping and open burning.

## Frontline Services

1. **Ecological Solid Waste Collection Management:** Formulating route schedules for municipal garbage compactor trucks and watercraft collection barges for coastal barangays (Binuangan and Salambao).
2. **Environmental Clearances for Businesses:** Mandatory inspection and clearance confirming waste segregation and grease trap / wastewater compliance for commercial establishments and food stalls.
3. **Materials Recovery Facility (MRF) Oversight:** Supporting barangay MRF operations and waste-to-resource exchange drives.
4. **Special & E-Waste Collection:** Coordinating safe drop-off and transfer of toxic chemicals, spent lead batteries, CFL bulbs, and electronic scrap to DENR-licensed TSD facilities.
5. **Environmental Law Enforcement:** Investigation of citizen complaints regarding illegal river dumping, open burning ("pagsisiga"), and unauthorized canal blockages.
6. **Coastal & Mangrove Rehabilitation:** Organizing community cleanups and mangrove (_bakawan_) planting missions along Obando and Binuangan riverbanks.

## Quick Transaction Checklist

- **For Environmental Clearance (Business):** DTI/SEC registration, Barangay Business Clearance, grease trap / waste storage details, and waste hauler contract (if high-volume generator).
- **To Report Environmental Violations:** Date, time, exact street or river location, and photographic/video evidence.
