# Municipal Engineering Office (MEO)

> Office of the Building Official responsible for infrastructure construction, building permits, and public works maintenance in Obando.

---

## Mandate & Overview

The **Municipal Engineering Office (MEO)** of Obando serves as the Office of the Building Official in accordance with Presidential Decree 1096 (The National Building Code of the Philippines). The office is tasked with the planning, design, construction, repair, and engineering supervision of municipal roads, bridges, drainage systems, floodgates, public markets, and municipal government buildings.

- **Office Head:** Municipal Engineer / Local Building Official
- **Location:** 2nd Floor, Obando Municipal Hall, J.P. Rizal St., Brgy. Paliwas, Obando, Bulacan 3021
- **Service Hours:** Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
- **Trunkline:** (02) 8292-2043

---

## Frontline Services

1. **Building Permit Processing:** Evaluation and approval of architectural, structural, sanitary/plumbing, electrical, and mechanical plans for all construction activities.
2. **Certificate of Occupancy:** Mandatory inspection and certification required before any newly constructed, renovated, or expanded structure can be legally occupied or energized.
3. **Ancillary Permits:** Issuance of Electrical Permits, Plumbing Permits, Demolition Permits, Fencing Permits, and Signboard Permits.
4. **Road and Drainage Maintenance:** Repair of municipal potholes, dredging of drainage culverts, and maintenance of tidal sluice gates.
5. **Annual Structural Safety Inspection:** Routine engineering check of commercial buildings, warehouses, and schools to prevent structural failures.

---

## Quick Transaction Checklist

- **For Building Permit:** Approved Locational Clearance from MPDO, 5 sets of engineering plans (signed & sealed by respective licensed engineers), land title / lease contract, structural computation, and BFP Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance (FSEC).
