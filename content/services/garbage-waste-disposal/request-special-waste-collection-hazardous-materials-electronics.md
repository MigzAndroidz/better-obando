# Request Special Waste Collection for Hazardous Materials and E-Waste in Obando

> How to safely surrender toxic household chemicals, fluorescent lamps, used batteries, electronics, and medical sharps to the Obando MENRO.

---

## Overview

Certain categories of household and commercial waste pose severe environmental, biological, and toxicity risks if thrown into general trash or dumped into Obando's sensitive water channels and fishpond dikes.

Under Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR) guidelines and **Republic Act 6969 (Toxic Substances and Hazardous and Nuclear Wastes Control Act)**, the **Obando Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office (MENRO)** coordinates specialized collection drives and safe drop-off mechanisms to ensure hazardous items and electronic wastes (e-waste) are transferred to DENR-licensed treatment, storage, and disposal (TSD) facilities.

---

## What Counts as Special or Hazardous Waste?

| Waste Classification           | Common Household & Commercial Examples                                                                                             | Environmental Danger                                                           |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **E-Waste (Electronic Scrap)** | Busted desktop computers, laptops, cellphones, chargers, CRT/LCD screens, audio equipment, microwave ovens, refrigerators, cables  | Contains heavy metals: lead, cadmium, mercury, and brominated flame retardants |
| **Household Chemical Waste**   | Leftover automotive paints, thinner, varnish, chemical strippers, rust converters, pesticides, pool chlorine, strong acid cleaners | Toxic vapors, water poisoning, severe burn hazard                              |
| **Mercury-Containing Devices** | Compact Fluorescent Lamps (CFL bulbs), fluorescent tubes, mercury fever thermometers, blood pressure gauges                        | Mercury vapor inhaled by waste handlers causes neurological damage             |
| **Spent Batteries**            | Lead-acid motorcycle and car batteries, rechargeable lithium-ion cells, alkalines, button cells                                    | Acid leakage and toxic heavy metal contamination in soil and groundwater       |
| **Household Medical Sharps**   | Used insulin syringes, lancets, testing strips, bloody bandages from home medical care                                             | Infectious needle-stick injuries and biological hazard to garbage collectors   |

---

## Safe Handling and Storage Guidelines at Home

- **Store Separately:** Never mix hazardous waste or chemicals with everyday food scraps or general trash bags.
- **Keep Intact:** Do not break fluorescent tubes or smash CRT television tubes, as this releases toxic mercury dust and gas into the room.
- **Safe Container for Sharps:** Place used insulin syringes and lancets in a puncture-proof plastic container (such as an empty heavy plastic bleach or detergent bottle with a secured cap). Never throw loose needles in soft trash bags!
- **Label Clearly:** Mark containers boldly with a permanent marker: `"DANGER: HAZARDOUS WASTE / TOXIC - DO NOT OPEN"`.
- **Store High and Dry:** Keep away from children, pets, and elevated above potential tidal floodwaters.

## Methods of Disposal in Obando

### 1. Quarterly Barangay E-Waste & Toxic Drop-Off Drives

MENRO conducts quarterly e-waste and special toxic collection drives in designated barangay centers. During these drives, residents can bring e-waste and spent batteries to their Barangay Hall for safe consolidation.

### 2. Direct Surrender to Obando MENRO

Residents and small business owners may surrender hazardous household items directly to the MENRO staging facility:

- **Venue:** Obando Municipal Hall Compound, Brgy. Paliwas.
- **Schedule:** Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM.
- **Procedure:** Log the items at the MENRO intake desk. An environmental officer issues an acknowledgment receipt for recorded toxic waste disposal.

### 3. Surrendering Used Household Needles & Medical Waste

Home dialysis or diabetic patients using syringes can turn over sealed puncture-proof needle containers to the **Obando Rural Health Unit (RHU)** in Paliwas for inclusion in the municipal infectious healthcare waste autoclave disposal stream.

---

## Bulk Commercial Hazardous Waste for Businesses

Automotive repair shops, welding fabrication yards, and manufacturing facilities in Obando are prohibited from using residential drop-off channels. They must:

1. Register as a Hazardous Waste Generator with the **DENR Environmental Management Bureau (EMB Region III)**.
2. Contract an accredited, DENR-licensed Hazardous Waste Transporter and TSD facility.
3. Submit hazardous waste transport permits and certificates of treatment during their annual Obando Mayor's Business Permit renewal.
