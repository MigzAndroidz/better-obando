# Emergency Response Procedures and Evacuation Routes in Obando

> Critical survival steps, evacuation centers, safe routes, and emergency hotlines during floods, typhoons, and storm surges in Obando, Bulacan.

---

## Overview

Due to its estuarine elevation near sea level, the **Municipality of Obando** is vulnerable to severe flooding triggered by a combination of high astronomical tides (Manila Bay), torrential rains from typhoons or the southwest monsoon (Habagat), and upstream river discharge from Bulacan and Rizal waterways.

Understanding your neighborhood's designated evacuation route, knowing when to preemptively evacuate, and knowing who to contact during an emergency can save lives.

## Evacuation Protocol Stages

The Obando MDRRMO issues color-coded flood and storm surge warning advisories:

```
[ALERT LEVEL 1: ADVISORY - Yellow]
Tidal height projected above 1.4 meters or monsoon rains forecasted.
Action: Monitor MDRRMO radio and social bulletins. Check household Go-Bag.
       ↓
[ALERT LEVEL 2: PREPARATION - Orange]
Tidal height projected between 1.6m – 1.8m. Low-lying roads become impassable to light vehicles.
Action: Preemptive evacuation of elderly, bedridden patients, and pregnant women.
Move household appliances to upper floors. Secure livestock and boats.
       ↓
[ALERT LEVEL 3: FORCED EVACUATION - Red]
Tidal height projected above 1.8 meters, severe storm surge, or river dike breach threat.
Action: MANDATORY EVACUATION. Move immediately along designated high-ground routes
to designated evacuation centers. Municipal rescue teams deploy boats to evacuate residents.
```

## Emergency Survival Protocols for Residents

### During Flood Inundation:

1. **Shut Off Main Utilities:** Switch off the main electrical breaker/fuse box before floodwaters reach electrical wall outlets to avoid electrocution. Shut off LPG tank valves.
2. **Never Walk or Drive Through Rushing Floodwaters:** Six inches of moving water can knock an adult off their feet; two feet of water can float a vehicle.
3. **Beware of Submerged Canals and Manholes:** Obando's road drainage culverts and open ditches become invisible beneath murky floodwaters. Use a walking stick to probe the pathway in front of you.
4. **Water Safety for Binuangan and Salambao:** Life jackets are mandatory when traveling by motorboat during storm conditions. Never board an overloaded passenger boat.

### If Trapped in Your Home:

- Move to the highest level or rooftop.
- Signal rescuers by waving a brightly colored cloth or flashlight.
- Call the **Obando Rescue 24/7 Dispatch Hotline: (02) 8281-5816**. Provide your exact street name, nearest house landmark, and number of trapped individuals.
