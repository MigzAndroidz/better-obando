# Participate in Disaster Drills and Community Evacuation Planning in Obando

> Community drills for coastal storm surges, river flooding, and earthquakes organized by the Obando MDRRMO and Barangay Disaster Committees.

---

## Overview

Due to its geographical exposure to Manila Bay storm surges, high-tide river backflow, and the West Valley Fault system running through Greater Metro Manila, the **Municipality of Obando** prioritizes regular community disaster preparedness drills and participatory evacuation planning.

Under Republic Act 10121 (Philippine Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act of 2010), the **Obando Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (MDRRMO)** organizes simulated flood evacuations, earthquake drills, and coastal water rescues across all 11 barangays, schools, and commercial establishments.

---

## Core Drills Conducted in Obando

### 1. Nationwide Simultaneous Earthquake Drill (NSED)

- Conducted quarterly in public schools, municipal offices, churches, and market centers.
- Focuses on the **"Duck, Cover, and Hold"** protocol, orderly evacuation to open open-field assembly areas, headcount accounting, and basic search-and-rescue response.
- Special evacuation scenarios account for potential liquefaction along river dikes and bridge crossings.

### 2. High-Tide and Storm Surge Flood Evacuation Drills

- Conducted in high-risk low-lying barangays, especially **Binuangan**, **Salambao**, **Paliwas**, and **Tawiran**.
- Simulates sudden tidal surges, motorboat water rescues, riverbank barrier reinforcement, and rapid transport of vulnerable individuals (infants, bedridden seniors, pregnant women) using municipal aluminum rescue boats.

### 3. School and Workplace Fire Evacuation Drills

- Held in partnership with the **Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) Obando Fire Station**.
- Teaches proper use of fire extinguishers (PASS method), electrical circuit isolation during floodwaters, and smoke navigation in high-density residential areas.

---

## How Citizens and Organizations Can Participate

### For Residents and Families:

- **Join Barangay Drills:** Participate actively when your Barangay Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Committee (BDRRMC) announces an evacuation drill. Walk the actual designated evacuation route to familiarize your children with safety pathways.
- **Formulate a Family Disaster Plan:** Establish an agreed meeting place in case family members are separated during a daytime flood or tremor.

### For Business Establishments and Fishpond Operators:

- Private firms, commercial stores along J.P. Rizal St., and fishpond enterprises are required to maintain an active **Emergency Action Plan** and conduct annual evacuation drills as part of their BFP fire safety and Mayor's Permit compliance.
- Request MDRRMO assistance to evaluate your building's emergency exit signage and flood evacuation protocols.

### For Community Volunteers (Rescue 8281):

- Sign up as an **Accredited Community Disaster Volunteer (ACDV)** with the Obando MDRRMO.
- Volunteers receive specialized training in basic life support (BLS), flood boat handling, community triage, and emergency radio communications.

## What to Bring During an Evacuation Drill (Go-Bag Checklist)

Every household in Obando is encouraged to maintain a ready **Emergency "Go-Bag" (Emergency Disaster Kit)** containing:

- [ ] Sealed bottled water (at least 3 liters per person for 3 days).
- [ ] Ready-to-eat non-perishable canned food and energy biscuits (with manual can opener).
- [ ] First aid kit and 7-day supply of maintenance medications.
- [ ] Battery-powered or crank radio and LED flashlights with spare batteries.
- [ ] Whistle for signaling rescuers.
- [ ] Waterproof pouch containing original identity documents, birth certificates, land titles, and cash.
- [ ] Multi-tool or pocket knife, power bank, and waterproof emergency blankets.
