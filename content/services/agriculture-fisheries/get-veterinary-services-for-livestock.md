# Veterinary Services, Livestock Care, and Pet Vaccination in Obando

> Free anti-rabies vaccination, livestock health monitoring, and veterinary assistance from the Obando Municipal Agriculture and Veterinary Desk.

---

## Overview

The **Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO)** of Obando, in coordination with the **Bulacan Provincial Veterinary Office (PVO)** and the Bureau of Animal Industry (BAI), provides veterinary health services to protect livestock, small backyard poultry, and companion animals throughout the municipality.

Maintaining animal health is vital to preventing zoonotic disease outbreaks such as rabies, avian influenza, and swine fever, thereby ensuring both food biosecurity and community safety in Obando.

---

## Veterinary Services Offered

### 1. Free Community Anti-Rabies Vaccination for Dogs and Cats

- Under Republic Act 9482 (Anti-Rabies Act of 2007), all pet owners must register and have their dogs and cats vaccinated annually against rabies starting at 3 months of age.
- **Mass Vaccination Campaigns:** Conducted door-to-door or in designated barangay covered courts in all 11 barangays during Rabies Awareness Month (March) and World Rabies Day (September).
- **Walk-In Pet Vaccination:** Available at the Municipal Agriculture Office on designated veterinary clinic days.
- **Rabies Certificate & Dog Tag:** Issued free upon successful vaccination.

### 2. Livestock & Backyard Poultry Care

- **Deworming and Vitamin Supplementation:** For backyard swine, goats, and ducks/poultry.
- **Disease Surveillance and Quarantine:** Regular testing and biosecurity inspections for African Swine Fever (ASF) and Avian Flu to protect smallholder raisers.
- **Veterinary Consultation & Prescription:** Diagnostic evaluation of sick farm animals with advice on medication and quarantine management.

### 3. Spay and Neuter (Kapon) Outreach Programs

- Periodic free or low-cost mass spaying and neutering for dogs and cats to humanely manage the stray animal population and prevent animal-bite incidents.
- Coordinated with the Provincial Veterinary Office and partner animal welfare veterinary missions.

---

## Documentary Requirements and Pet Preparation

### For Dog and Cat Vaccination:

- The animal must be at least **3 months old**.
- The animal must be healthy, active, and free from fever, cough, or severe skin mange on the day of vaccination.
- Female animals must not be pregnant or actively nursing.
- Bring the pet's existing **Pet Vaccination Card / Health Record** if available.
- Dogs must be leashed or muzzled; cats must be placed inside a secure carrier, net, or pillowcase to prevent escapes and scratching.

### For Livestock Consultation:

- Proof of Obando residency (Barangay Clearance or Valid ID).
- Exact location of backyard pens or piggery/poultry facility.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

```
[Step 1: Check Clinic Schedule or Barangay Visit]
Inquire at the Municipal Agriculture Office or follow barangay bulletin announcements.
       ↓
[Step 2: Animal Registration & Vitals Check]
Pet or animal details logged in the municipal rabies / veterinary registry.
Attending veterinarian checks temperature and general health status.
       ↓
[Step 3: Administration of Vaccine or Treatment]
Vaccine or dewormer administered by licensed provincial/municipal veterinarian.
       ↓
[Step 4: Certificate Issuance]
Owner receives an official Rabies Vaccination Certificate indicating vaccine batch
and expiration date. Keep this certificate safely for travel and bite emergencies!
```
