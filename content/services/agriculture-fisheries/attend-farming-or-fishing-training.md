# Attend Farming and Fishery Training Seminars in Obando

> Skills training, modern aquaculture management, post-harvest fish processing, and sustainable livelihood seminars offered by the Obando Municipal Agriculture Office.

---

## Overview

To boost the productivity and economic resilience of local producers, the **Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO)** of Obando conducts regular training seminars and technical workshops in collaboration with the **Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR Region III)**, the **Department of Agriculture (DA)**, and **TESDA**.

These practical training courses empower Obando's fisherfolk, fishpond workers, women's cooperatives, and youth with modern aquaculture practices, water quality management, and value-adding fish processing skills.

---

## Training Programs Offered

### 1. Modern Aquaculture and Fishpond Management

- **Bangus and Tilapia Culture Techniques:** Optimal stocking densities, feed conversion ratio (FCR) improvement, and natural feeding practices (lablab, plankton propagation).
- **Water Quality Monitoring & Disease Prevention:** Testing salinity, dissolved oxygen (DO), pH, and ammonia levels; preventing mass fish kills during seasonal hot-cold water inversions and sudden torrential rain downpours.
- **Polyculture and High-Value Species:** Integrating mud crab (alimango) fattening and giant tiger prawn (sugpo) polyculture in traditional bangus ponds.

### 2. Post-Harvest Seafood Value Addition

- **Smoked Fish (Tinapa) & Dried Fish Processing:** Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP), food safety, brine curing, and modern hygienic smokehouse operation.
- **Fish Deboning and Bangus Processing:** Value-added deboning of fresh bangus, marinated daing na bangus, and bangus belly fillets for institutional buyers.
- **Bottled Sardines & Seafood Condiments:** Canning and bottling techniques for gourmet fish delicacies and fermented shrimp paste (alamang/bagoong).

### 3. Sustainable Urban Agriculture and Hydroponics

- Backyard container gardening and vertical farming for flood-prone spaces.
- Organic composting from fish offal and kitchen waste.
- Rainwater harvesting and greywater filtration for home vegetable patches.

---

## Who Can Join the Training?

- Registered fisherfolk (FishR holders) and fishpond workers in Obando.
- Members of local rural improvement clubs (RIC), women's livelihood associations, and cooperatives.
- Displaced workers and out-of-school youth seeking skills for micro-enterprises.
- All bona fide residents of Obando interested in starting home food production.

**Training Cost:** 100% Free. All instructional materials, lab ingredients, and training certificates are subsidized by the LGU and partner national agencies.

---

## How to Register for an Upcoming Training

1. **Visit the Agriculture Office:** Inquire about upcoming training modules at the **Municipal Agriculture Office**, 2nd Floor, Obando Municipal Hall, Paliwas.
2. **Submit Registration Form:** Complete the Training Enlistment Form with your contact details, barangay address, and livelihood background.
3. **Bring Requirements:**
   - One (1) valid ID showing Obando residence.
   - Barangay Clearance or Fisherfolk Certificate (if applying for specialized aquaculture courses).
4. **Attend the Orientation and Workshop:** Complete attendance across all lecture and hands-on laboratory modules to qualify for graduation.
5. **Receive Certificate of Completion:** Issued jointly by the LGU, BFAR, and partner agencies, which can be used to qualify for LGU livelihood starter kits or microfinance loans.
