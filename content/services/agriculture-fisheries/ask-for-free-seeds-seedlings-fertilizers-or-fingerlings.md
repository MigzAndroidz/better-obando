# Request Free Seeds, Seedlings, Fertilizers, and Fingerlings in Obando

> How local fisherfolk, fishpond operators, and backyard gardeners can avail of aquaculture fingerlings and vegetable inputs from the Obando Municipal Agriculture Office.

---

## Overview

The **Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO)** of Obando supports the town's agricultural and fishing communities by providing free and subsidized production inputs. Because Obando's terrain is predominantly composed of fishponds, brackish estuaries, and riverbanks along Manila Bay, local agricultural assistance is heavily focused on **aquaculture production** (bangus, tilapia, sugpo/prawn, and mud crabs) alongside urban gardening and backyard crop cultivation.

Programs are implemented in partnership with the **Department of Agriculture (DA) Region III**, the **Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR)**, and the **Provincial Agriculture Office of Bulacan**.

---

## What Production Inputs Are Available?

### 1. Aquaculture Fingerlings and Crablets (BFAR / MAO Coordinated):

- **Milkfish (Bangus) Fry / Fingerlings:** Distributed during the seasonal stocking cycle for brackish water fishponds.
- **Tilapia Fingerlings (Saline-tolerant strains):** Suitable for low-to-medium salinity waters in Obando's inland waterways.
- **Mud Crab (Alimango) Crablets & Prawn (Sugpo) Post-Larvae:** Provided to accredited fisherfolk cooperatives for high-value pond diversification.

### 2. Vegetable Seeds and Urban Agriculture Starter Kits:

- Certified vegetable seed packets (Pinakbet varieties: eggplant, sitaw, ampalaya, okra, squash; lowland tomatoes, pechay, mustasa).
- Organic compost fertilizers and bio-stimulants for home gardens and barangay communal vegetable plots.
- Fruit-bearing tree seedlings suitable for coastal saline soil (duhat, talisay, coconut, calamansi).

---

## Who is Eligible to Avail?

- **Registered Obando Fisherfolk:** Must be enrolled in the **Fisherfolk Registration (FishR)** database.
- **Registered Aquaculture Operators:** Licensed fishpond operators with an active Obando business permit or lease agreement.
- **Farmers and Backyard Gardeners:** Residents of Obando with available backyard space or participating in barangay communal gardens.
- **LGU-Accredited Fisherfolk and Farmers Cooperatives:** Recognized community associations in Obando (e.g., in Binuangan, Salambao, Tawiran, Paliwas).

---

## Documentary Requirements

To request fingerlings or agricultural inputs, submit the following:

- [ ] Duly accomplished **Agricultural / Fishery Input Request Form**.
- [ ] Copy of valid government ID with Obando residence.
- [ ] **Barangay Certification** verifying that the applicant is an active fishpond operator, fisherfolk, or gardener in the barangay.
- [ ] Copy of **FishR Registration Card** (for aquaculture fingerlings).
- [ ] For fishpond operators: Approximate pond area (in hectares), pond location, and desired stocking density.

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

```
[Step 1: Application Filing]
Visit the Obando Municipal Agriculture Office at the Municipal Hall in Paliwas.
Complete the Request Form indicating target pond location or garden area.
       ↓
[Step 2: Technical Assessment & Water Quality Check]
MAO fisheries technician verifies FishR status and assesses pond readiness
(checking salinity, depth, and predator-free status).
       ↓
[Step 3: Scheduling of Distribution]
Fingerling releases are consolidated and scheduled based on BFAR hatchery supply runs.
Recipients are informed 3 to 5 days prior to arrival.
       ↓
[Step 4: Acclimatization & Turnover]
Recipients bring oxygenated containers or clean buckets to the designated landing station.
Fisheries technician supervises water temperature acclimatization prior to stocking.
```
