# Borrow Farm and Aquaculture Equipment from the Obando LGU

> Lending procedures for water quality test meters, water pumps, sprayers, and post-harvest tools provided by the Obando Municipal Agriculture Office.

---

## Overview

Purchasing specialized aquaculture and agricultural machinery can require significant capital investment for individual smallholders and artisanal fisherfolk. To support local production efficiency, the **Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO)** of Obando maintains an equipment pool available for scheduled borrowing by accredited fisherfolk associations, cooperatives, and registered farmers.

This equipment-sharing initiative ensures small operators have access to essential tools for water quality control, pond desiltation, post-harvest processing, and disaster recovery after flooding.

---

## Available Equipment for Borrowing

- **Aquaculture Water Quality Testing Kits:** Digital hand-held refractometers (salinity meters), dissolved oxygen (DO) meters, and pH testing kits for pond condition monitoring.
- **Portable Water Pumps & Submersible Dewatering Units:** High-volume motorized pump sets for emergency fishpond draining, flood pumping, or water replenishment during low tide cycles.
- **Knapsack Sprayers & Power Sprayers:** For applying biological probiotic pond treatments or organic fertilizers.
- **Fish Processing & Deboning Kits:** Stainless steel deboning tables, vacuum packaging sealers, and impulse heat sealers for certified food processing groups.
- **Weighing Scales (Heavy-Duty Platform Scales):** For cooperative fish harvest weighing and bulk marketing.

---

## Eligibility and Borrowing Guidelines

- **Priority Given to Cooperatives:** Registered fisherfolk organizations, Barangay Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Management Councils (BFARMC), and recognized livelihood associations have first priority.
- **Individual Borrowers:** Registered fishpond operators (FishR cardholders) may borrow hand-held testing meters or pumps upon endorsement by their Barangay Captain.
- **Security & Care Agreement:** The borrower signs an Equipment Custody Receipt agreeing to handle machinery with due care, supply their own fuel and motor oil, and return tools in clean, working condition within the agreed timeframe (typically 1 to 5 days).

---

## Step-by-Step Borrowing Procedure

```
[Step 1: Check Equipment Availability]
Inquire at the Municipal Agriculture Office at the Municipal Hall in Brgy. Paliwas.
Verify that the requested equipment is in stock and operational.
       ↓
[Step 2: Submit Written Request Form]
Submit a Borrowing Request Form at least 3 working days prior to target use date.
Attach: Barangay Endorsement, valid government ID, and FishR card copy.
       ↓
[Step 3: Equipment Testing & Release]
MAO property officer tests the machine with the borrower to verify working status.
Borrower signs the Property Custody and Equipment Acknowledgement Receipt.
Safety and operational briefing provided.
       ↓
[Step 4: Return and Return Inspection]
Return the equipment on or before the due date, cleaned and drained of excess water.
Officer signs off on return clearance.
```
