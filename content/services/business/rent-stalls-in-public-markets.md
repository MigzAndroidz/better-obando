# Lease Stalls in the Obando Public Market

> Procedure for applying, leasing, and operating commercial stalls within the Obando Public Market and LGU economic enterprise facilities.

---

## Overview

The **Obando Public Market**, situated in the town center, is managed by the **Municipal Economic Enterprise Development Office (MEEDO)** under the Office of the Municipal Mayor. The market accommodates vendors across dry goods, wet market sections (fresh fish, bangus, tilapia, crabs, seafood, meat, poultry), vegetables, native delicacies, and cooked food carinderias.

Public market stalls are leased to qualified Filipino citizens and bona fide residents of Obando on a contractual lease basis under the provisions of the Obando Market Code and Municipal Revenue Ordinance.

---

## Eligibility Criteria

To apply for a vacant public market stall in Obando, the applicant must:

1. Be a Filipino citizen of legal age (at least 18 years old).
2. Be a bona fide resident of the Municipality of Obando, Bulacan (proven through Voter's Certification or Barangay Certificate of Residency).
3. Have no pending delinquent accounts, unpaid lease rentals, or unliquidated obligations with the Obando Municipal Treasury.
4. Not be an existing holder of more than the maximum permitted stall units (as designated by the Market Code to prevent monopolization).

---

## Market Sections Available for Lease

- **Wet Section:** Fresh marine and aquaculture fish (bangus, tilapia, kanduli, hito), shrimps, crabs, pork, beef, and dressed chicken.
- **Dry Section:** Rice and grain retailers, groceries, spices, processed food, and general merchandise.
- **Vegetable and Fruit Section:** Fresh highland and lowland produce, native root crops.
- **Cooked Food / Carinderia Section:** Prepared meals, breakfast delicacies, native snacks.
- **Apparel and Footwear Section:** Clothes, slippers, tailoring, and household supplies.

---

## Documentary Requirements

Submit two (2) sets of the following documents to the Market Master's Office:

- [ ] Duly accomplished **Market Stall Lease Application Form**.
- [ ] **Barangay Clearance** for commercial lease from applicant's barangay of residence.
- [ ] **Certificate of Residency** or Voter's Certification proving bona fide residence in Obando.
- [ ] Photocopy of two (2) valid government-issued IDs (e.g., PhilSys National ID, Driver's License, Postal ID, Passport).
- [ ] Two (2) recent 2x2 ID photos with white background.
- [ ] **Medical and Health Certificate** issued by the Obando Rural Health Unit (RHU), including stool examination, chest X-ray, and Hepatitis screening (mandatory for wet and cooked food sections).
- [ ] **Community Tax Certificate (Cedula / CTC)** issued by the Obando Municipal Treasury for the current year.

## Application Procedure

### Step 1: Inquire About Stall Vacancy

Visit the **Market Master's Office** at the Obando Public Market to verify the availability of vacant stalls in your desired section. Notice of vacant stalls is publicly posted on the market bulletin board and the Municipal Hall information kiosk.

### Step 2: Submission and Verification

Submit your accomplished application form and required documents to the Market Master. The office verifies residency, assesses qualification, and checks the applicant's credit record with the Municipal Treasury.

### Step 3: Public Bidding or Raffle Award

When multiple qualified applicants apply for the same prime stall, the **Market Committee** convenes a public lottery or bidding process in accordance with municipal guidelines to ensure fair and transparent awarding.

### Step 4: Execution of Lease Contract

Upon selection, the applicant signs the **Contract of Lease for Public Market Stall** with the Municipal Mayor. The contract outlines:

- Monthly rental rates per square meter as defined by the Municipal Revenue Code.
- Permitted line of merchandise or goods.
- Operating hours and cleanliness obligations.
- Prohibitions against subleasing, assignment, or unauthorized stall alterations.

### Step 5: Advance Deposit and Security Settle

Pay the required **advance lease rental** and **security deposit** at the Municipal Treasury Cashier. Obtain official receipts.

### Step 6: Securing Mayor's Permit and Issuance of Stall Key

Present the signed lease contract and treasury receipts to the BPLO to process the Market Vendor Mayor's Permit and Sanitary Permit. The Market Master inspects the unit and officially turns over the stall key.

---

## Key Rules and Vendor Responsibilities

- **Subleasing Strictly Prohibited:** Lessees are strictly prohibited from subleasing, selling, transferring, or mortgaging their stall rights to third parties. Any unauthorized transfer results in automatic contract revocation and forfeiture of deposits.
- **Daily Cleanliness & Solid Waste Segregation:** Stallholders must maintain segregated trash bins (biodegradable and non-biodegradable) and turn over waste to market sweepers following Obando MENRO standards.
- **Weighing Scale Accuracy:** All wet and dry market scales must be calibrated and sealed by the Municipal Treasury. Public test scales ("Timbangan ng Bayan") are available inside the market for consumer protection.
