# Access Potable Water Supply and Public Deep-Well Systems in Obando

> How to apply for residential piped water connections, public tap stands, and water quality testing in the Municipality of Obando.

---

## Overview

Access to clean, safe, and potable drinking water is essential for public health. In the **Municipality of Obando**, piped potable water distribution is primarily serviced by **Maynilad Water Services** (West Zone concessionaire) across mainland barangays, complemented by **Barangay Level II Water Systems** (communal faucets) and deep-well public tap stands in outlying coastal and island settlements like **Brgy. Binuangan** and **Brgy. Salambao**.

The **Municipal Health Office (RHU Sanitation Division)** and the **Municipal Engineering Office (MEO)** monitor drinking water quality, bacteriological safety, and communal water infrastructure across the town.

---

## 1. Applying for a New Piped Water Connection (Maynilad)

Residents in mainland Obando barangays (Paliwas, Panghulo, Catanghalan, Paco, Lawa, San Pascual, Tawiran, Hulo, Pag-asa) can apply for individual residential water service:

### Documentary Requirements:

- [ ] Duly accomplished **Maynilad Water Service Application Form**.
- [ ] Proof of Ownership or Right of Occupancy:
  - If owner: Transfer Certificate of Title (TCT), Deed of Absolute Sale, or Real Property Tax Declaration.
  - If tenant/renter: Signed Lease Contract and Lessor's Written Authorization with copy of owner's valid ID.
- [ ] **Barangay Clearance** for water service installation from the host barangay.
- [ ] **Excavation Permit** issued by the Obando Municipal Engineering Office (if installation requires cutting concrete road pavement or sidewalk).
- [ ] Two (2) valid government-issued IDs of the applicant.

### Application Procedure:

1. Visit the nearest Maynilad Business Area Office or apply online via the Maynilad portal.
2. An engineering technician conducts a line inspection to determine mainline proximity, water pressure, and excavation needs.
3. Pay connection fees and meter deposit.
4. Meter installation and water service activation typically completed within 7 to 10 working days after permit approval.

## 2. Communal Water Tap Stands & Deep-Well Access in Coastal Areas

In coastal island communities (such as Binuangan and Salambao) where deep saline groundwater is brackish and piped mainland connections are geographically constrained:

- **Barangay Desalination / Communal Filtration Stations:** The LGU and partner agencies maintain solar-assisted reverse osmosis (RO) filtration systems or communal rainwater harvesting storage tanks.
- **Potable Water Barge Deliveries:** During dry summer months or when local storage is depleted, the municipal government coordinates clean drinking water deliveries via watercraft to community storage reservoirs.
- **Public Tap Stands:** Maintained by the Barangay Council for shared household domestic use.

---

## 3. Water Quality and Bacteriological Testing

To prevent waterborne diseases such as cholera, typhoid, and acute gastroenteritis:

- The **Obando Rural Health Unit (RHU) Sanitation Inspector** collects monthly water samples from public tap stands, commercial water refilling stations, and school water faucets for bacteriological testing.
- **Commercial Water Refilling Stations:** Required to display updated monthly **Microbiological Water Test Results** (Coliform / E. coli analysis) and secure an updated **Sanitary Permit** from the Obando RHU to operate legally.
