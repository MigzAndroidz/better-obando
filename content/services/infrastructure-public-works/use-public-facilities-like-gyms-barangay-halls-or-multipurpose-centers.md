# Use Public Facilities: Gyms, Multi-Purpose Halls, and Covered Courts in Obando

> Reservation guidelines, booking fees, and permits for using municipal gymnasiums, evacuation centers, and barangay multi-purpose facilities.

---

## Overview

The **Municipality of Obando** maintains various community facilities, sports complexes, and multi-purpose covered courts available for civic assemblies, youth sports leagues, cultural presentations, community weddings, and private gatherings.

Public facilities are managed under the supervision of the **General Services Office (GSO)**, the **Office of the Municipal Mayor**, and respective **Barangay Councils**.

---

## Major Municipal and Barangay Public Facilities

### 1. Municipal Facilities:

- **Obando Municipal Gymnasium / Multi-Purpose Covered Court:** Located near the Municipal Hall compound in Brgy. Paliwas. Accommodates sports leagues, cultural concerts, trade exhibitions, and municipal assemblies.
- **Paco Multi-Purpose Evacuation Center & Sports Complex:** A major covered facility in Brgy. Paco designed for high-capacity community events, athletic training, and emergency disaster shelter.
- **Obando Town Plaza & Historical Grounds:** Located in Brgy. Pag-asa (Poblacion) fronting the Sanctuary of San Pascual Baylon. Available for cultural festivals, religious processions, and civic ceremonies.

### 2. Barangay Covered Courts & Multi-Purpose Halls:

- Individual covered courts and community halls located in each of Obando's 11 barangays (Binuangan, Catanghalan, Hulo, Lawa, Paco, Pag-asa, Paliwas, Panghulo, Salambao, San Pascual, Tawiran).
- Available for local youth basketball/volleyball leagues, barangay general assemblies, vaccination drives, and private family celebrations.

---

## Reservation Guidelines and Scheduling

### Prioritization Hierarchy:

1. **Official LGU and Disaster Operations:** First priority. (During declared storm alerts, facilities designated as evacuation centers automatically suspend all private bookings).
2. **Barangay Government & Public School Activities:** DepEd district meets, SK sports tournaments, and barangay assemblies.
3. **Socio-Civic and Religious Community Events:** Church activities, charity medical missions, and NGO forums.
4. **Private and Commercial Events:** Birthday celebrations, sports club practices, and corporate assemblies.

---

## Application Procedure to Book a Facility

```
[Step 1: Inquire About Date Availability]
Visit the General Services Office (GSO) at the Municipal Hall (for municipal facilities)
or the respective Barangay Hall (for barangay courts) at least 10 working days in advance.
       ↓
[Step 2: Submit Written Letter of Request]
Address formal letter to the Municipal Mayor (for municipal gym) or Barangay Captain.
Include: Event title, target date and time duration, estimated attendees,
and equipment required (sound system, lighting, plastic chairs).
       ↓
[Step 3: Assessment of Rental & Maintenance Fees]
GSO or Barangay Treasurer computes standard utility and janitorial maintenance fee
as prescribed by the Municipal Revenue Code.
(Official school and government events are exempt from rental charges).
       ↓
[Step 4: Treasury Payment & Permit Issuance]
Pay fees at the Municipal Treasury Cashier (or Barangay Treasury).
Receive the approved Facility Reservation Permit.
```

## Rules of Use and Borrower Responsibilities

- **Clean As You Go (CLAYGO):** Borrowers are strictly responsible for cleaning up all litter, food scraps, and banners after their event. Waste must be sorted into biodegradable and recyclable bins.
- **No Smoking and No Alcohol Policy:** Under municipal ordinance, smoking, vaping, and consuming alcoholic beverages are strictly prohibited inside all public government sports and assembly facilities.
- **Equipment Care:** Any damage to basketball backboards, electronic scoreboards, sound systems, stage fixtures, or glass windows must be repaired or replaced at the borrower's expense.
- **Curfew & Decibel Limits:** Nighttime events must conclude and lower sound system volume in compliance with municipal noise ordinances (typically by 10:00 PM on weekdays).
