# Public Market Stalls and Municipal Cemetery Services in Obando

> Procedures for public market leases, cemetery niche rental, burial permits, and exhumations managed by the Obando Municipal Government.

---

## Overview

The **Municipal Economic Enterprise Development Office (MEEDO)** and the **Municipal Treasury** oversee local government-managed economic facilities in Obando. These include commercial stalls within the **Obando Public Market** and burial services at the **Obando Municipal Public Cemetery**.

These public facilities are operated to provide affordable commercial spaces for local entrepreneurs and dignified, orderly final resting places for deceased citizens of Obando.

---

## 1. Obando Public Market Stall Leasing

The Obando Public Market offers commercial stall spaces across designated dry, wet, and food sections under lease contracts:

### Summary of Requirements:

- Filipino citizenship and bona fide residence in Obando (Barangay Certificate and Voter's Certificate).
- Market Stall Lease Application Form submitted to the **Market Master's Office**.
- Valid government ID, Community Tax Certificate (Cedula), and 2x2 photos.
- Sanitary Health Clearance issued by the Obando Rural Health Unit (RHU) for food and wet market handlers.
- Contract signing with the Municipal Mayor and payment of advance deposit and monthly rental at the Municipal Treasury Cashier.

_(For detailed step-by-step guidance on market stalls, see the dedicated [Lease Stalls in the Obando Public Market](/services/business/rent-stalls-in-public-markets) guide)._

## 2. Municipal Public Cemetery Services

The **Obando Municipal Cemetery** provides burial niches, apartment-type tombs, and ground plots for deceased bona fide residents.

### Services Offered:

- **Burial Permit Issuance:** Mandatory municipal authorization required prior to any interment.
- **Apartment-Type Niche Lease:** Standard 5-year lease terms for public concrete burial niches, renewable upon expiration or subject to bone transfer.
- **Exhumation and Bone Transfer Permits:** Authorization to exhume remains after the mandatory 5-year burial period for cremation, transfer to ossuary niches, or family mausoleums.
- **Cemetery Maintenance and Security:** Oversight of public pathways, perimeter fencing, and lighting.

### Requirements for Burial Permit and Niche Lease:

1. Certified True Copy of the **Death Certificate** registered and stamped by the Obando Local Civil Registry.
2. **Barangay Certification of Residency** of the deceased (and claimant).
3. Valid government ID of the immediate family member or legal claimant.
4. If from a hospital/embalming facility: Transfer of Cadaver Permit (if the person died outside Obando).
5. Payment of **Burial Permit Fee** and **Cemetery Niche Rental Fee** at the Municipal Treasury Cashier.

---

## Exhumation and Bone Transfer Guidelines

Under DOH sanitation rules and the Code on Sanitation of the Philippines (PD 856):

- Remains may be exhumed only after **five (5) full years** from the date of burial.
- The applicant files an Exhumation Application Form with the **Municipal Health Office (RHU Sanitation Division)**.
- An exhumation permit is issued by the Municipal Health Officer and Municipal Mayor.
- Disinterment must be conducted respectfully under the supervision of the Cemetery Caretaker and RHU Sanitary Inspector.
