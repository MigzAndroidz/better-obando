# Join Livelihood and Skills Training Programs in Obando

> Community skills training, TESDA technical-vocational courses, and livelihood capital grants offered by Obando MSWDO and the Public Employment Service Office (PESO).

---

## Overview

The **Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)** and the **Public Employment Service Office (PESO)** of Obando provide community-based livelihood and vocational skills training to empower indigent families, solo parents, persons with disabilities, out-of-school youth, and displaced workers.

Courses are offered free of charge in coordination with **TESDA Bulacan**, the **Department of Trade and Industry (DTI)**, and regional non-government training partners. Training emphasizes income-generating activities tailored to local markets, including seafood processing, garment sewing, electronics repair, and culinary arts.

---

## Courses and Training Modules Offered

### 1. Community Food Processing and Culinary Skills

- Commercial production of smoked fish (tinapa), dried fish (daing), and gourmet bottled seafood.
- Commercial bread and pastry baking, native kakanin production, and commercial catering basics.
- Food safety and sanitation protocols compliant with DOH and FDA standards.

### 2. Garment, Crafts, and Cottage Industries

- Commercial dressmaking, curtain fabrication, and basic tailoring.
- Souvenir and novelty creation for religious pilgrims attending the Obando Fertility Dance Festival (embroidered towels, hats, rosary beads, miniature icons).
- Upcycling fabric scraps and reusable plastics into eco-bags and rugs.

### 3. Technical, Trade, and Digital Skills

- Small engine maintenance and motorboat engine repair (vital for coastal fishermen in Binuangan and Salambao).
- Residential electrical installation and maintenance (EIM NC II).
- Basic computer literacy, social media marketing, and freelance virtual assistance basics for youth.

---

## Starter Kits and Micro-Livelihood Assistance

Graduates of designated MSWDO/PESO skills courses are eligible for:

- **Livelihood Starter Toolkits:** Basic equipment (such as sewing machines, food processing kits, or baking tools) awarded to top-performing graduates.
- **Sustainable Livelihood Program (SLP):** Organization of community beneficiaries into **Self-Help Groups (SHGs)** or Sustainable Livelihood Associations (SLAs) with access to seed capital grants funded by DSWD and the LGU.
- **Trade Fair Participation:** Free booth spaces in municipal trade fairs and Sunday markets sponsored by the Obando Negosyo Center.

---

## Eligibility and Enlistment

### Who Can Apply:

- Bona fide resident of the Municipality of Obando.
- At least 18 years of age.
- Priority given to:
  - 4Ps beneficiary household members.
  - Registered Solo Parents.
  - Displaced or informal sector workers.
  - Out-of-school youth and persons with disabilities.

### Requirements:

1. Copy of valid government ID with Obando address.
2. Barangay Certificate of Indigency or Residency.
3. Two (2) recent 1x1 ID pictures.
4. Completed Livelihood Training Application Form.
