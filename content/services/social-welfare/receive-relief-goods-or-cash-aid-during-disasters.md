# Disaster Relief Goods and Emergency Cash Assistance in Obando

> Procedures for food pack distribution, temporary shelter assistance, and emergency relief during high tides, typhoons, and calamities in Obando, Bulacan.

---

## Overview

Due to its geographical location along Manila Bay and the convergence of local river tributaries, the Municipality of Obando is susceptible to tidal flooding, storm surges, and monsoon inundation.

When severe weather disturbances, heavy rainfall, or extreme astronomical high tides threaten community safety, the **Obando Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council (MDRRMC)** and the **Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)** activate the **Municipal Emergency Relief Operations** to provide essential family food packs, potable drinking water, hygiene kits, and emergency financial assistance.

---

## Standard Relief Goods Provided

- **Family Food Packs (FFPs):** Typically contains 6 kg of rice, canned goods (corned beef, tuna, sardines), instant coffee sachets, and fortified instant noodles.
- **Potable Water Supply:** 5-gallon purified drinking water jugs distributed directly to evacuation centers and isolated coastal barangays (Binuangan and Salambao).
- **Family Hygiene Kits:** Bath soap, laundry detergent bars, toothbrushes, toothpaste, sanitary pads, and bath towels.
- **Emergency Sleeping Kits:** Thermal blankets, plastic ground mats (banig), and mosquito nets for evacuees housed in designated evacuation centers.

---

## Relief Operations Channels

### 1. In Designated Evacuation Centers

Evacuees registered in primary municipal and barangay evacuation facilities (such as the Paco Evacuation Center, Obando Central School, and Barangay Multi-Purpose Halls) are automatically provided hot meals, bottled water, and sleeping supplies upon registration with the MSWDO camp manager.

### 2. House-to-House Barangay Distribution

For residents marooned by high floodwaters who remain inside their two-story homes:

- Barangay officials and Tanods, assisted by the Obando MDRRMO Rescue boat teams, conduct door-to-door watercraft distribution of relief food packs using the master list of household heads.
- Special relief supply trips via motorboat are dispatched from the Paliwas river landing to the island communities of **Binuangan** and **Salambao**.

---

## Emergency Financial Assistance (AICS Disaster Cash Aid)

In the aftermath of declared state of calamity or catastrophic events (such as structural fires, dike breaches, or severe typhoon damage):

- **Emergency Shelter Assistance (ESA):** Cash assistance provided to families whose homes were categorized as **totally damaged** or **partially damaged**, verified by MSWDO and the Municipal Engineering Office.
- **Burial & Medical Assistance for Disaster Victims:** Immediate financial grants to families of deceased or hospitalized disaster victims.

### Requirements to Avail of Disaster Cash Assistance:

1. Valid government-issued ID of the claimant.
2. **Barangay Certification of Disaster Damage / Evacuation Record**.
3. **Disaster Victim Disaster Assessment and Response (DROMIC) Intake Sheet** prepared by the MSWDO social worker.
4. Photographs of the damaged residential house showing house number and street landmark.
5. In case of fire: Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) Obando Fire Incident Certificate.
