# Apply for Senior Citizen, Solo Parent, or PWD Assistance in Obando

> Procedures for obtaining official IDs, statutory purchase discounts, and municipal social assistance benefits for seniors, single parents, and persons with disabilities.

---

## Overview

The **Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)** of Obando, together with the **Office of Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA)** and the **Persons with Disability Affairs Office (PDAO)**, administers statutory benefits and welfare support in accordance with national laws:

- **Republic Act 9994:** Expanded Senior Citizens Act of 2010
- **Republic Act 10754:** Act Expanding the Benefits and Privileges of Persons with Disability
- **Republic Act 11861:** Expanded Solo Parents Welfare Act of 2022

Frontline processing takes place at the **Social Services Wing**, Ground Floor, Obando Municipal Hall, Brgy. Paliwas.

---

## 1. Senior Citizen Identification Card & Booklet (OSCA)

### Benefits:

- 20% discount and VAT exemption on medicines, doctor consultations, diagnostic exams, hospitalization, public transportation, restaurants, recreation, and basic groceries.
- Mandatory PhilHealth coverage under the National Health Insurance Act.
- Eligibility for the national Social Pension for Indigent Senior Citizens (₱1,000 monthly, disbursed quarterly).
- Centenarian cash gift (₱100,000 national gift plus municipal recognition upon reaching 100 years of age).

### Requirements:

1. Proof of age: Original PSA Birth Certificate (or Local Civil Registry copy) or valid Philippine Passport showing date of birth.
2. Proof of residency: **Barangay Certificate of Residency** stating at least six (6) months residence in Obando.
3. Two (2) recent 1x1 ID pictures with white background.
4. Old government-issued ID (e.g., Voter's ID, Driver's License, SSS/GSIS ID).

---

## 2. Persons with Disability (PWD) ID Card

### Benefits:

- 20% discount and VAT exemption on medicines, medical/dental supplies, transport fares, dining, and hotel accommodations.
- Special parking spaces and designated accessible priority lanes in public and private commercial establishments.
- Free municipal assistive devices (wheelchairs, walking canes, crutches) subject to MSWDO stock availability and indigent assessment.

### Requirements:

1. **Medical Certificate / Clinical Assessment:** Issued by an attending government or private physician clearly stating the diagnosis and specific type of disability (e.g., visual, hearing, orthopedic/physical, psychosocial, intellectual, speech, or chronic illness).
2. Barangay Certificate of Residency in Obando.
3. Two (2) recent 1x1 ID pictures.
4. Accomplished PWD Application Form (available at the MSWDO / PDAO desk).

---

## 3. Solo Parent Identification Card

### Benefits:

- Seven (7) working days of paid **parental leave** per year (for formally employed solo parents who have rendered at least one year of service).
- Flexible work schedule options provided by employers under RA 11861.
- Monthly cash subsidy of **₱1,000 per child** for qualified minimum-wage or low-income solo parents (subject to DSWD / LGU guidelines and budget allocation).
- 10% discount and VAT exemption on baby's milk, food, vitamins, and medical supplies for children aged 0 to 6 years old (for solo parents earning less than the minimum wage).
- Educational scholarship priority for solo parents and their dependent children.

### Requirements:

1. Proof of status as a solo parent (any of the following):
   - Death certificate of spouse.
   - Court decree of nullity of marriage or legal separation.
   - Medical certification of spouse's permanent incapacity or mental incompetence.
   - Certificate of Detention (if spouse is incarcerated for more than 3 months).
   - Sworn Affidavit of Abandonment by spouse for at least six (6) months.
   - Birth certificate(s) of child/children with unacknowledged paternity.
2. PSA Birth Certificate(s) of dependent child/children (below 22 years old, unmarried and unemployed, or with PWD condition).
3. Barangay Certificate of Residency in Obando stating solo parent status.
4. Proof of financial status: Latest ITR, Certificate of Indigency, or Certificate of Low Income.
5. Two (2) recent 1x1 ID pictures of the applicant.

## Step-by-Step Application Flow

```
[Step 1: Document Pre-Check]
Visit the MSWDO / OSCA / PDAO counter at the Obando Municipal Hall (Ground Floor).
Officer checks completeness of documentary requirements.
       ↓
[Step 2: Social Worker Intake & Interview]
A registered social worker conducts a brief assessment interview
and encodes applicant details into the national/municipal database.
       ↓
[Step 3: Verification & Approval]
The MSWDO Head or OSCA Head signs the endorsement and ID authorization.
       ↓
[Step 4: ID Card Releasing]
Cardholder claims the laminated ID card and official Purchase Booklet for medicines/groceries.
Valid nationwide in all participating commercial establishments.
```
