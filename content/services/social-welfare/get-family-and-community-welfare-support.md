# Family and Community Welfare Support (AICS) in Obando

> Procedures for availing of Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situations (AICS), medical grants, burial assistance, and family counseling at Obando MSWDO.

---

## Overview

The **Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)** of Obando serves as the frontline social protection arm of the municipal government. Through the **Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situations (AICS)** program, social case management, and family protective services, the MSWDO provides critical financial, psychosocial, and legal support to vulnerable residents facing severe hardships.

Services are provided directly at the **MSWDO Office**, Ground Floor, Obando Municipal Hall, Brgy. Paliwas.

---

## Direct Crisis Assistance (AICS)

### 1. Medical and Hospitalization Assistance

- **Financial Grants for Inpatient Hospital Bills:** Guarantee letters or direct checks issued to accredited public hospitals (Valenzuela Medical Center, Meycauayan District Hospital, Bulacan Medical Center) to offset outstanding balances.
- **Prescription Medicine & Diagnostic Aid:** Subsidies for high-cost chemotherapy drugs, hemodialysis sessions, MRI/CT scans, and specialized laboratory tests.
- **Requirements:**
  - Original Clinical Abstract / Medical Certificate signed by the attending physician (with license number).
  - Original Hospital Billing Statement (for confinement) or official Doctor's Prescription with quotation.
  - Barangay Certificate of Indigency of the patient or claimant.
  - Valid ID of patient and representative.

### 2. Burial and Funeral Assistance

- **Financial Aid for Deceased Indigents:** Cash assistance or municipal funeral service vouchers to help indigent families cover casket, mortuary, and interment costs.
- **Requirements:**
  - Certified true copy of the **Death Certificate**.
  - Original **Funeral Contract / Official Receipt** from the funeral parlor.
  - Barangay Certificate of Indigency of the claimant.
  - Proof of relationship to the deceased (Marriage Certificate for spouse, Birth Certificate for children/parents).
  - Valid ID of claimant.

### 3. Transportation Assistance

- For stranded individuals or displaced families needing to return safely to their home provinces in the Philippines.
- **Requirements:** Police blotter (if victim of crime/theft) or Barangay certification, valid ID, and itinerary.

## Family and Child Protection Services

The MSWDO also leads specialized social intervention units:

- **Women and Children Protection Desk (WCPD Coordination):** Social workers provide crisis intervention, temporary shelter referrals, psychological counseling, and legal assistance for victims of domestic violence (RA 9262) and child abuse (RA 7610).
- **Children in Conflict with the Law (CICL):** Diversion programs, restorative justice interventions, and parental counseling under the Juvenile Justice and Welfare Act (RA 9344).
- **Pre-Marriage Counseling (PMC):** Mandatory family planning and marital preparation seminar conducted jointly with the Obando Rural Health Unit for couples applying for a marriage license.

## Step-by-Step Procedure to Avail of AICS

```
[Step 1: Intake & Document Verification]
Proceed to the MSWDO Waiting Area at the Municipal Hall in Paliwas.
Submit required documents (Medical abstract, funeral contract, indigency) to the intake worker.
       ↓
[Step 2: Social Case Study Interview]
A registered Municipal Social Worker conducts a brief interview
to assess family socio-economic status and immediate needs.
       ↓
[Step 3: Approval & Social Case Study Report]
Social worker prepares the assessment voucher and endorses it to the MSWDO Head
and Municipal Mayor for final authorization.
       ↓
[Step 4: Treasury Disbursement]
Proceed to the Municipal Treasury Cashier to claim the approved financial assistance check
or cash disbursement, or receive a formal Guarantee Letter for the hospital.
```
