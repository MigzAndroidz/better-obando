# Apply for Locational Clearance, Zoning, and Building Permits in Obando

> Procedures for securing Locational Clearances from the MPDO and Building Permits from the Municipal Engineering Office in Obando, Bulacan.

---

## Overview

All residential, commercial, industrial, and institutional construction, renovation, addition, or demolition projects in the **Municipality of Obando** require statutory clearances in compliance with Presidential Decree 1096 (The National Building Code of the Philippines) and the Comprehensive Land Use Plan (CLUP) and Zoning Ordinance of Obando.

Regulatory processing involves two sequential stages:

1. **Locational Clearance (Zoning):** Issued by the **Municipal Planning and Development Office (MPDO) / Zoning Administrator** to verify that the proposed construction complies with municipal land use classifications, building setbacks, and river legal easements.
2. **Building Permit:** Issued by the **Municipal Engineering Office / Building Official** after structural, architectural, electrical, sanitary/plumbing, mechanical, and fire safety engineering plans are thoroughly vetted.

---

## Stage 1: Securing Locational Clearance (Zoning)

Before submitting building plans to the Building Official, secure a Locational Clearance from the Zoning Administrator:

### Requirements:

- [ ] Duly accomplished **Locational Clearance Application Form** (3 copies).
- [ ] Proof of Land Ownership:
  - Certified True Copy of the **Transfer Certificate of Title (TCT)** issued by the Registry of Deeds within the last 3 months.
  - Current **Real Property Tax Declaration** and official receipt of updated tax payment.
  - If applicant is not the registered owner: Notarized **Contract of Lease**, Deed of Absolute Sale, or Special Power of Attorney (SPA) from the owner.
- [ ] Five (5) sets of complete Architectural Site Development and Location Plans signed and sealed by a licensed Civil Engineer or Architect.
- [ ] **Barangay Construction Clearance** issued by the host barangay.
- [ ] Environmental Compliance Certificate (ECC) or Certificate of Non-Coverage (CNC) from the DENR-EMB (for commercial or industrial developments).

### Location & Processing:

- Submit to the **MPDO Zoning Desk**, 2nd Floor, Obando Municipal Hall, Paliwas.
- Zoning officers verify property zoning (e.g., Low-Density Residential, High-Density Commercial, Industrial, or Aquaculture Buffer Zone).
- Upon payment of zoning fees at the Municipal Treasury, the **Locational Clearance** is issued.

---

## Stage 2: Securing the Building Permit

With your Locational Clearance approved, submit the complete engineering plan docket to the Office of the Building Official (MEO):

### Engineering Document Docket (Five [5] Sets, Signed & Sealed):

- [ ] Complete **Architectural Plans** (Floor plans, elevations, sections, schedules).
- [ ] **Structural Plans** and Structural Design Analysis & Computation (for structures 2 stories and above, certified by a licensed Civil/Structural Engineer).
- [ ] **Electrical Plans**, Single-Line Diagram, and Load Computation (signed & sealed by a Professional Electrical Engineer - PEE).
- [ ] **Sanitary / Plumbing Plans** and Septic Tank Detail (signed & sealed by a Master Plumber / Sanitary Engineer).
- [ ] **Mechanical Plans** (for elevators, air-conditioning plants, or generators, signed by a Professional Mechanical Engineer - PME).
- [ ] **Estimated Bill of Materials and Cost Specifications**.
- [ ] Soil Boring Test / Geotechnical Investigation Report (for structures 3 stories and above).
- [ ] **Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance (FSEC)** issued by the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) Obando Fire Station.

---

## Application and Inspection Workflow

```
[Step 1: Locational Clearance Filing]
File application with the MPDO Zoning Desk.
Site inspection verifies property line setbacks and minimum river easement compliance.
       ↓
[Step 2: Building Permit Intake & Joint Review]
Submit five (5) plan sets to the Municipal Engineering Office.
Engineers review: Architectural, Structural, Electrical, and Sanitary compliance.
Simultaneously routed to BFP Obando for Fire Safety Evaluation (FSEC).
       ↓
[Step 3: Assessment of Municipal Building Fees]
Building Official computes building permit fees based on floor area and building classification
(pursuant to the National Building Code schedule of fees).
       ↓
[Step 4: Treasury Payment]
Settle assessed building permit and regulatory fees at the Municipal Treasury Cashier.
       ↓
[Step 5: Issuance of Building Permit]
Building Official signs and releases the Building Permit along with approved stamped plan sets.
Construction may legally commence!
```

---

## Important Notice on Obando River Easements

> **Legal Easement Rule:** Pursuant to Presidential Decree 1067 (Water Code of the Philippines) and Obando municipal ordinances, a mandatory legal easement strip (minimum of 3 meters along urban rivers and 20 meters along agricultural/aquaculture waterways) measured from the high-water shoreline must remain strictly free from permanent structures to facilitate public flood control and emergency dike access.
