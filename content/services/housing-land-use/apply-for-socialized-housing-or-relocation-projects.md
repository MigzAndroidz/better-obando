# Apply for Socialized Housing and Relocation Programs in Obando

> Housing beneficiary profiling, National Housing Authority (NHA) resettlement, and socialized housing programs in the Municipality of Obando.

---

## Overview

Due to its estuarine river geography and high risk of tidal storm surges, the **Municipality of Obando**, through the **Local Housing Board (LHB)**, the **Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)**, and the **Municipal Planning and Development Office (MPDO)**, actively coordinates socialized housing programs and safe relocation initiatives for informal settler families (ISFs) living along danger zones.

In partnership with the **National Housing Authority (NHA)**, the **Department of Human Settlements and Urban Development (DHSUD)**, and national socialized housing initiatives (such as the 4PH - Pambansang Pabahay para sa Pilipino Program), the LGU facilitates secure, permanent housing with basic utilities for vulnerable Obando residents.

---

## Priority Beneficiary Categories

Under Republic Act 7279 (Urban Development and Housing Act of 1992):

- **Families in Danger Zones:** Households residing along riverbank easements (within the 3-meter to 10-meter river legal easement), estuarine dikes, and floodway channels of the Obando and Binuangan rivers.
- **Displaced Families by Infrastructure Projects:** Families affected by government flood control dike construction, bridge expansions, or river dredging operations.
- **Low-Income and Indigent Families:** Bona fide residents living in rented, makeshift, or shared dwellings with no legal land tenure or real property registered in their name.

---

## Qualification Criteria for Socialized Housing

To qualify for municipal socialized housing or resettlement endorsement:

1. Must be a Filipino citizen of legal age (or head of family).
2. Must be a bona fide resident of the Municipality of Obando, Bulacan (proven through census tagging, barangay residency, or voter's record).
3. Must **not** own any other residential land or housing unit anywhere in the Philippines.
4. Must **not** be a professional squatter or member of a squatting syndicate (as defined by law).
5. Must have a monthly family income within the statutory low-income housing threshold.

---

## Documentary Requirements

Prepare two (2) sets of the following documents:

- [ ] Accomplished **Socialized Housing Beneficiary Application Form**.
- [ ] Copy of valid government ID of applicant and spouse.
- [ ] **Barangay Certificate of Residency & Indigency** confirming actual duration of residence and lack of real property ownership.
- [ ] Copy of **PSA Marriage Certificate** (if married) or PSA Birth Certificates of dependent children.
- [ ] Proof of income: Certificate of Employment with compensation, latest ITR, or Barangay Certificate of Low Income / Self-Employment (e.g., fisherfolk, tricycle driver, vendor).
- [ ] **Official Tagging / Census Slip** issued during LGU / NHA household surveys along danger zones (if applicable).
- [ ] Sworn Affidavit of Non-Land Ownership executed before a notary public.

## Application and Verification Flow

```
[Step 1: Census Tagging & Beneficiary Profiling]
LGU Local Housing Board conducts tagging and socio-economic survey in target barangays.
Eligible household heads receive official tagging stencils and control numbers.
       ↓
[Step 2: Document Submission & Social Case Intake]
Applicant submits documentary packet to the MSWDO / Local Housing Desk in Paliwas.
A registered social worker evaluates family needs and verifies eligibility.
       ↓
[Step 3: Verification with Registry of Deeds (RD)]
Verification conducted to confirm applicant does not hold titled property in Bulacan or Metro Manila.
       ↓
[Step 4: Housing Pre-Qualification & Site Matching]
Qualified beneficiaries are pre-screened for available NHA relocation communities
or national socialized housing development projects in Bulacan.
       ↓
[Step 5: Pre-Relocation Orientation & Awarding]
Beneficiaries attend mandatory social preparation and financial literacy seminars
prior to signing housing amortizations and receiving unit keys.
```
