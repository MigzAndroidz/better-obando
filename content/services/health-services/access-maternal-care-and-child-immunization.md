# Access Maternal Care & Child Immunization in Obando

> Guidelines for prenatal care, post-natal checkups, family planning counseling, and the Expanded Program on Immunization (EPI) in the Municipality of Obando.

---

## Overview

The **Obando Municipal Health Office** ensures safe motherhood and healthy child development through comprehensive maternal and child health programs. All expectant mothers and young children in Obando have access to quality, free health services delivered through the **Obando Rural Health Unit (RHU)** in Brgy. Paliwas and the 11 **Barangay Health Stations (BHS)**.

Our community public health midwifes, public health nurses, and Barangay Health Workers (BHWs) actively track expectant mothers and infants to reduce infant and maternal mortality across our coastal and inland barangays.

---

## Maternal Healthcare Services

### 1. Free Prenatal Care

Every pregnant woman in Obando is encouraged to complete at least **four (4) prenatal visits** starting in the first trimester:

- **First Visit (1st Trimester - Up to 12 weeks):** Complete obstetric history, physical exam, baseline blood pressure and weight, maternal risk screening, and initial prenatal blood/urine screening requests (CBC, Blood Typing, Urinalysis, VDRL, HBsAg, Fasting Blood Sugar).
- **Second Visit (2nd Trimester - 13 to 27 weeks):** Fetal heartbeat monitoring, fundic height measurement, nutritional check, and Tetanus-Diphtheria (Td) immunization.
- **Third Visit (3rd Trimester - 28 to 36 weeks):** Assessment of fetal presentation, gestational diabetes screening review, birth planning counseling, and PhilHealth enrollment verification.
- **Fourth Visit (36 weeks to Delivery):** Delivery plan finalization, facility-based delivery endorsement, emergency preparedness, and danger signs counseling.

### Free Maternal Supplements:

- **Ferrous Sulfate + Folic Acid** tablets (to prevent maternal anemia and birth defects).
- **Calcium Carbonate** supplements.
- **Tetanus-Diphtheria (Td) Toxoid** immunization to safeguard mother and newborn against tetanus infection.

### 2. Facility-Based Delivery Endorsement

Home deliveries by traditional birth attendants (hilot) are strictly discouraged under municipal health ordinances to protect maternal and newborn lives. The Obando RHU endorses delivering mothers to accredited public birthing clinics, district hospitals (such as Meycauayan General Hospital or Valenzuela Medical Center), or accredited private maternity lying-in clinics.

### 3. Postpartum Care and Newborn Screening

- **Postnatal Visits:** Conducted within 24–48 hours and 7 days after delivery by the barangay midwife or public health nurse to assess maternal healing, lactation, and newborn wellbeing.
- **Newborn Screening (NBS):** Mandatory blood spot test collected within 24 to 72 hours from birth to detect congenital metabolic disorders.
- **Newborn Hearing Screening:** Referral to accredited regional pediatric centers.

## Child Immunization (Expanded Program on Immunization - EPI)

Routine childhood immunizations protect children against serious infectious diseases and are provided **100% free of charge**.

### Official National Immunization Schedule:

| Child's Age              | Vaccine Administered               | Disease Prevention                                                      |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **At Birth**             | BCG (Bacillus Calmette-Guérin)     | Severe forms of Tuberculosis (meningitis, miliary)                      |
| **At Birth**             | Hepatitis B (within 24 hours)      | Hepatitis B virus infection and chronic liver damage                    |
| **6 Weeks (1½ months)**  | Pentavalent 1, OPV 1, PCV 1        | Diphtheria, Pertussis, Tetanus, Hep B, Hib, Polio, Pneumonia/Meningitis |
| **10 Weeks (2½ months)** | Pentavalent 2, OPV 2, PCV 2        | Booster protection for the 6-week series                                |
| **14 Weeks (3½ months)** | Pentavalent 3, OPV 3, PCV 3, IPV 1 | Full initial series, plus Inactivated Poliovirus injection              |
| **9 Months**             | Measles-Rubella (MR 1), IPV 2      | Measles, Rubella (German Measles), Polio boost                          |
| **12 Months (1 Year)**   | Measles-Mumps-Rubella (MMR 2)      | Long-term immunity against Measles, Mumps, Rubella                      |

### Where and When to Bring Your Baby:

- **Regular Immunization Day:** Every **Wednesday**, 8:00 AM – 3:00 PM.
- **Venues:** Obando RHU Main (Paliwas) and individual Barangay Health Stations across Obando (Binuangan, Catanghalan, Hulo, Lawa, Paco, Pag-asa, Paliwas, Panghulo, Salambao, San Pascual, and Tawiran).
- **Document Needed:** Bring your child's **Early Childhood Care and Development (ECCD) Card / Baby Book** at every visit. (A new ECCD card is issued free on your first visit).

## Family Planning and Reproductive Health

The Obando RHU provides free, confidential counseling and modern family planning commodities to couples and individuals based on informed choice:

- **Pills:** Combined oral contraceptives and progestin-only pills (for lactating mothers).
- **Injectables:** Depo-Medroxyprogesterone Acetate (DMPA / 3-month injectable).
- **Subdermal Implants:** 3-year contraceptive rod insertions (by trained RHU certified providers).
- **Intrauterine Devices (IUD):** Postpartum or interval IUD insertion.
- **Barrier Methods:** Free distribution of condoms.
- **Natural Family Planning Counseling:** Standard Days Method, Billings Ovulation Method, Lactational Amenorrhea Method (LAM).
- **Permanent Methods:** Referral to partner hospitals for Bilateral Tubal Ligation (BTL) or Non-Scalpel Vasectomy (NSV).
